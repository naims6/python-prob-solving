RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
RAG retrieves relevant information from external documents and provides that information to a language model before it generates an answer.

Embeddings are numerical vector representations of text.
Texts with similar meanings can have similar embeddings, depending on the embedding model.

Chunking divides a large document into smaller pieces.
Chunk size and overlap can affect retrieval quality.

A vector database stores vectors and supports similarity search.
It helps applications find relevant information without comparing every document manually.
